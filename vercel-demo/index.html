What do the dashes like “needs paid data” mean?

Some details (team size, valuations, growth) only exist in paid databases like PitchBook or Coresignal, which this demo doesn't subscribe to. Rather than guessing, the tool leaves those cells empty and says why — an honest blank beats a made-up number. Everything that IS shown comes from a real, linked source.
